Skyline: a walk along the world's tallest buildings

Nobody can feel “828 metres” — scroll sideways from an ordinary Canberra house to the Burj Khalifa and feel it instead.

The ground never leaves; instead the whole world zooms out to keep the next building on screen, so the house you started at shrinks, in front of you, from a home to a speck. Heights are each building's architectural top (the CTBUH measure — spire included, antenna excluded), stated once here so the numbers mean the same thing throughout. The three Canberra stops are typical, illustrative figures derived from standard residential dimensions, not measurements of a real address.
